      About the exercises

      Thought record

      A structured way to slow down a moment that stirred up strong feelings. You write down the situation, the emotions and the thought behind them, then look at what supports and doesn't support the thought, and write a more complete version. Re-rating at the end shows whether anything shifted for you.

      Behavioral experiment

      A way to test a belief by acting on it. You write a specific prediction, plan a small experiment, try it, and compare what happened with what you expected. You can start one from any thought record with “Test it with an experiment”.

      Your data

      Everything stays in this browser on this computer. Export a JSON backup regularly from Import / Export: browser storage can be cleared without warning.

      Self-test

      Runs against an in-memory journal and a temporary IndexedDB database. Your journal is not read or changed.
